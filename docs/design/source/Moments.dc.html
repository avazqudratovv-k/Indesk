9 · Moments

Small animations for moments people remember

Sign-up, payment, finished runs, failures, rejected files. Pure CSS and SVG: no libraries, no scripts, about 13 KB. They loop here so you can review them; in the product each plays once. They stop for people who prefer reduced motion.
